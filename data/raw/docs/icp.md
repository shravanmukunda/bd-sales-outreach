Webknot Technologies Pvt. Ltd. sells software delivery to companies that already have a product and need engineers to build it. A useful lead is a company with a public reason to talk now: they are hiring developers, they just raised a round, or they launched a product that will need a team behind it.

## Who is a fit

The company is building or scaling a software product, not shopping for a one-off brochure site. Typical stages are seed through Series C. India is the home market, and Bengaluru shows up often because that is where many product teams hire. Companies outside India are still a fit when they are hiring into India or want a product engineering partner there.

Signals that are worth a conversation:

- An open role for React, Node, mobile, or backend engineers on a public job page.
- A funding announcement that implies the product team will grow.
- A product launch that expands the surface area the team has to maintain.

Services to match against the lead:

- Product engineering squads that can own a feature from spec to release.
- Web and mobile application development, including React front ends.
- Dedicated engineers who join an existing product team.
- AI features only when the company is already shipping software and the feature is part of that product.

## Who is not a fit

Skip companies with no public business signal. Skip pure staff-augmentation requests that do not name a product. Skip anything that would require collecting personal contact details of employees. The agent may use public company pages, public job posts, and public news. It may not scrape personal inboxes, personal phone numbers, or private social profiles.
